# Glass Dome Showroom artwork

The five cars were generated with Codex's image tool (one image per call, 1254 × 1254 px, transparent background). Each was then cropped to the car, scaled to 720 px wide and saved as lossy WebP with alpha (`cwebp -q 84 -alpha_q 92`). The same files are bundled in the Swift and Kotlin apps. The cars, their design and their names are invented: no brand, logo, badge, plate or text.

The prompts below are the exact prompts sent to the tool; only the paint differs.

## car-cobalt.webp

Generated with Codex's image tool.

```text
Use your image generation tool to create one image: a compact modern electric crossover hatchback car of an invented design, painted in deep glossy cobalt blue metallic paint, with black-tinted windows, dark grey multi-spoke alloy wheels and a smooth closed front, in an exact side view (pure 90-degree profile, no perspective) with the front of the car facing right, camera at wheel-hub height, the whole car centered and filling 92 percent of the image width, both wheels resting on the same horizontal line at 70 percent of the image height, soft studio lighting from above with a crisp highlight line along the shoulder, photoreal product render, on a fully transparent background with no floor, no shadow, no reflection, square. No logos, no badges, no emblems, no license plates, no text, no people. Then copy the generated PNG into the current directory as car-cobalt.png and print its path and pixel size.
```
