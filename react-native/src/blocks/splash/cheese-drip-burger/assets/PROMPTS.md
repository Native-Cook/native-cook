# Cheese Drip Burger artwork

The burger was generated with Codex's image tool (one image per call, 1254 × 1254 px, transparent background). It was scaled to 900 × 900 px and saved as lossy WebP with alpha (`cwebp -q 80 -alpha_q 85 -m 6 -sharp_yuv`, 228 KB). The same file is bundled in the Swift and Kotlin apps. The cheese slices in the photo are flat on purpose: every drip, drop and puddle of molten cheese is drawn in code on top of them. The burger is invented: no brand, logo or text.

The prompt below is the exact prompt sent to the tool.

## burger.webp

Generated with Codex's image tool.

```text
Use your image generation tool to create one image: a photoreal tall double cheeseburger, stacked from bottom to top: a toasted brioche bottom bun, a thick seared juicy beef patty, a flat square slice of bright orange-yellow American cheese, a second thick seared beef patty, a second flat square slice of bright orange-yellow American cheese, crisp green lettuce, two red tomato slices, thin white onion rings, and a tall glossy golden sesame brioche top bun. The cheese slices are perfectly flat and only peek out a little at the edges as straight square corners, with NO melting, NO drips and NO sauce running down anything. Front view, camera slightly from above, the burger centered and filling about 80 percent of the image width, warm studio lighting from the upper left with a soft rim light, crisp clean edges, food advertising photography look, on a fully transparent background with no plate, no floor, no shadow, square. No text, no letters, no logos, no people. Then copy the generated PNG into the current directory as burger.png and print its path and pixel size.
```
