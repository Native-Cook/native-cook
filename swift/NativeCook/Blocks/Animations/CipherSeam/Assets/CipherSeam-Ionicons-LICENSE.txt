CipherSeam-Ionicons.ttf is Ionicons.ttf from @expo/vector-icons 15.1.1
(build/vendor/react-native-vector-icons/Fonts/Ionicons.ttf), the file the React Native
version of Cipher Seam draws its seven icons with (wifi, menu, chevron-forward,
reorder-three, sparkles, radio-button-on, chatbox).
